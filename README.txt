Little Rock Construction Estimator PWA v2

Features:
- Multiple wall sections
- 2 1/2, 3 5/8, 6, 8 and 10 inch stud selections
- 25, 20 and 18 gauge selections
- 12, 16 and 24 inch spacing
- Doors and windows tied to wall sections
- Drywall assemblies and one/two-sided walls
- Material pricing and labor production rates
- Overhead, profit, tax and bid calculation
- Saved projects stored locally on the device
- Little Rock supplier/reference tab
- Print / Save PDF customer bid
- PWA manifest and offline service worker

Deploy the files to an HTTPS host. Then open the site on iPhone or Android and use
Add to Home Screen / Install. Verify plans, specifications, assemblies, rates and
supplier quotes before issuing a contractual bid.
