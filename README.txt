Azteca Elite Construction Estimator v4.1 - Blueprint Takeoff & Review

Replace the existing GitHub Pages files with index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, and icon.svg.

V4.1 keeps the V4 estimator and adds a stronger human-review gate for blueprint findings: sheet classification, candidate dimensions, wall-type/opening findings, editable wall candidates, sides, assembly flags, confidence, and an explicit Reviewed step before candidates can be applied to Areas.

PDF.js is loaded from cdnjs when the blueprint reader is used. Image uploads remain visual references and require manual verification.

Do not use automated findings as a contractual bid without reviewing plans, specifications, assemblies, dimensions, scale, field conditions, supplier quotes and production rates.
